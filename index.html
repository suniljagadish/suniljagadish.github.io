6 sessions

RPE 6–7
This phase is about learning the movements, not loading them. Every set stops around RPE 6 to 7, roughly three reps in reserve. The loads are deliberately conservative because the point of these three weeks is pattern quality and tissue tolerance. Progression comes later.

How to use this

Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
Test the alarm before your first session. Button is at the top of every page. It uses your phone speaker, so the ringer switch has to be off silent. Tap a rest timer to start, tap it again to cancel, and it will sound and flash when the rest is up.
Session B moved to Saturday in week 1 only. Friday 14 August is the Squamish ride with Rich. Weeks 2 and 3 stay on Friday.
Session A loads come from the Onboarding block as planned in TrainingPeaks. Session B loads are proposed starting points rather than prescribed figures, so treat week 1 as a calibration session and adjust from there.

Plate maths assumes pairs of 45, 35, 25, 10, 5 and 2.5 lb plates. Trap bars vary between 45 and 60 lb, so set yours on each Session page.
